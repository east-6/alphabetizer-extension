How to use Alphabetizer
Quick start
  1. Paste or type your list, one item per line.
  2. Click Alphabetize.
  3. Click the copy icon at the top right to copy the result.

The arrow icon next to it undoes any step. Sorting follows the language of your text automatically, so accented and Turkish letters land in the right place with nothing to configure.

Change how it sorts
  1. Click Options.
  2. Pick A to Z or Z to A, and turn on Ignore case, Sort by number (puts item2 before item10), or Remove empty lines.
  3. Click Alphabetize again. Re-sorting with new settings is always safe.

To sort by a later part of each line, raise Sort from character. At 5, Dr. Smith sorts by Smith, and the character each line sorts by gets a box in your text.

Remove duplicate lines
  1. Click Options, then press Remove next to Remove duplicates.
  2. Click See details to check what was removed, or the small undo button beside Remove to bring everything back.
Change the text itself
  1. Click Options and look under Text output.
  2. Pick a Transform (uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, trim, collapse spaces, remove accents, slugify), turn on List numbering, or type Custom text to add to the start or end of every line.
  3. Click Alphabetize to apply.

Working with comma separated items instead of lines? Type the separator, like a comma, into Delimiter.

Find and replace
  1. Click the sliders button to the right of Options.
  2. Type into Find. Matches highlight in your text immediately; jump between them with the arrows or Enter.
  3. Toggle Aa for match case, ab for whole words, .* for regex.
  4. Type the replacement, then press Replace selection for the current match or Replace all.
Compare two lists
  1. Click the sliders button, then turn on Compare mode. A second field appears.
  2. Paste the second list and press Compare. Added and removed lines show like a code diff.
  3. Use the Alphabetize toggle in the header to sort both lists too, the arrows above the diff to swap sides, and Exit comparison mode when done.
More tools in Advanced options

Clean list markers: press Clean to strip bullets and numbering like "- item" or "1. item" from a pasted list. Randomize list: press Shuffle. Group by letter: turn it on and sorting adds A, B, C headings.

Outside the popup
  1. Select text on any web page.
  2. Right-click it and choose Alphabetize selection.
  3. The sorted text is now on your clipboard, ready to paste.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Enter sort, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+C copy, Ctrl+Shift+L open this popup.

Under General you can turn off Remember text if you don't want your text kept between sessions, or turn on Auto-copy result to skip the copy click. Everything runs locally. Your text never leaves your device.
